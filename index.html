🇹🇭 为什么泰国人都有小名？ Why Do Thai People Have Nicknames?

在泰国文化中，几乎每个人都会有一个从小由父母起的“小名”（泰语：ชื่อเล่น，Nickname）。由于泰文全名通常较长且发音复杂，泰国人在日常社交和职场中都习惯直接使用简短响亮的小名。

In Thai culture, almost everyone has a nickname (Thai: ชื่อเล่น) given by their parents at birth. Because formal Thai names are often long and difficult to pronounce, Thai people prefer using short and catchy nicknames for daily social and professional interactions.

Thai nicknames are inspired by a wide range of things, including:

  • 动物与自然 (Animals & Nature): เช่น/e.g., “นก” (Nok, Bird), “ฟ้า” (Fah, Sky).
  • 水果与食物 (Fruits & Food): เช่น/e.g., “แตงโม” (Tang-mo, Watermelon), “เค้ก” (Cake).
  • 现代外来词 (Modern & Loanwords): เช่น/e.g., “Benz” (奔驰), “Bank” (银行).
